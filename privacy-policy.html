Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 7, 2026 | Last Updated: October 7, 2026

Welcome to Readable – Stories & Tales ("Readable", "we", "us", or "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Android mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Readable is designed to respect your privacy. We do not require you to create a traditional user account with personal identifiers like your name or email address. Instead, we use an Anonymous Device Identifier to save your preferences.

2. Anonymous Device Identifier

When you install and open the App, we generate a unique, anonymous identifier tied to your specific device. This identifier is saved locally on your device (using SharedPreferences) and is used to sync your reading experience.

3. Reading History and Bookmarks

To provide a seamless reading experience, we store data related to your interaction with the stories in our MongoDB database using your anonymous device identifier. This includes:

  • Bookmarks: Stories you have saved for later.
  • Reading Progress: The stories you have opened, your scroll progress, and whether you have completed a story.

This data does not contain personally identifiable information (PII) and cannot be linked back to your real identity.

4. Analytics and App Usage

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the App. This helps us improve the user experience. Firebase Analytics may collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage data, such as screens viewed, buttons tapped, and general app engagement.

5. Crash Reporting

To maintain app stability, we use Firebase Crashlytics. If the App crashes, Crashlytics automatically collects crash reports, which may include details about your device model, operating system version, and the state of the App at the time of the crash. This data is strictly used for debugging and improving app reliability.

6. Advertising

For users on the free tier, we display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and other diagnostic data to serve relevant ads. By using the free version of the App, you consent to the collection and use of this data by Google for advertising purposes. You can opt out of personalized advertising through your Android device settings.

7. Premium Purchases

If you choose to purchase a Readable Premium subscription (e.g., $3.99/month or $29.99/year), the transaction is processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not collect, process, or store your credit card or payment information. We only receive a confirmation receipt from Google to unlock your premium features (such as an ad-free experience).

8. Data Storage (MongoDB)

The stories, your bookmarks, and your reading progress are securely stored in our cloud-hosted MongoDB database. The data is associated only with your anonymous device identifier.

9. Third-Party Services

The App utilizes the following verified third-party services:

  • Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics)
  • Google AdMob (Advertising)
  • Google Play Billing (In-App Purchases)
  • MongoDB (Data Storage)

These services operate under their respective privacy policies.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your anonymous reading history and bookmarks for as long as you use the App. Since we do not require a user account, if you uninstall the App and clear your device storage, a new anonymous identifier will be generated upon reinstallation, and your previous data will be orphaned and eventually purged. If you wish to request deletion of data associated with a specific device identifier, please contact us.

11. Children's Privacy

Readable is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us, and we will take steps to delete it.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
